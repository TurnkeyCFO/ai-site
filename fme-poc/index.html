How to demo it

Open the shop view and submit an order. Switch to the warehouse view and pick it, short an item if you like. Move to the driver view, deliver the stop and capture a signature. Then open operations and watch the same order arrive in the invoicing queue with its full chain of custody. One movement ledger underneath, so nothing can drift.

About this demo

Everything here runs in your browser on sample data: invented prices, invented quantities, real workflow. State is saved locally so your actions carry across the five views; the reset button in the black bar returns the demo to its starting point. The production system described in the proposal adds the QuickBooks interface, real logins per person, and your live catalog.

Built as a working companion to the Turnkey AI proposal, so every claim in the document is something you can click.
