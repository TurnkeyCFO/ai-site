In plain English

A missed call text back service is an automation that watches your business phone line and, the moment a call goes unanswered, sends that caller a friendly text within seconds. Where we're honest with you

What missed call text back won't do.

It's a simple tool that does one job extremely well. We'd rather you know the edges than oversell it. Here's where the line sits.

01 The honest limit It doesn't answer the phone for you.

It's a follow-up, not a live conversation

A live answer is still better than a text — some callers want to talk right now. Missed call text back is the safety net for the calls a person can't get to, not a replacement for picking up. If you're missing a lot of calls, pairing it with an AI receptionist that actually answers is usually the stronger setup, and we'll tell you that on the call.

02 The honest limit Someone still has to reply to the thread.

It opens the door — your team or a tool walks through it

The text-back starts the conversation, but a person who replies "still water in the basement?" closes more than an automation guessing at it. We can layer in automated replies and booking where it makes sense, but a text thread no one ever answers is no better than a voicemail no one checks. We make sure the replies land where your team will see them.

03 The honest limit Sometimes the fix is simpler than software.

If a better phone routine solves it, we'll say so

Some shops are dropping calls because of a clunky phone tree or a voicemail box that's always full — not because they need a new tool. On the free call we'll point that out if it's true. We make our money on great fits, not on selling automation a business doesn't need.

Common questions

What owners ask about missed call text back.

What is a missed call text back service? +

It's an automation that watches your business phone line and, the moment a call goes unanswered, sends the caller a friendly text within seconds — something like "Sorry we missed you, this is Smith Plumbing. How can we help?" The caller replies by text, and you've kept a lead that would otherwise have dialed the next company on Google.

How fast does the text go out after a missed call? +

Within a few seconds — fast enough that the caller usually still has their phone in hand. Speed is the whole point. A text that lands while they're still deciding who to call keeps the conversation with you; a text an hour later arrives after they've already booked someone else.

Will it annoy my customers or feel spammy? +

Not when it's done right. The text is short, sounds like your business, and only fires on a genuine missed call — not on every call, and not on numbers you've already spoken to that day. We tune the wording with you so it reads like a real person following up, because that's what a caller expects after they couldn't get through.

Does it replace my receptionist or office staff? +

No. It's a safety net for the calls your team can't get to — after hours, on a job, during the lunch rush. Your people still handle the live conversations and the booking. The text-back just makes sure a missed call turns into a text thread instead of a lost customer.

Will it work with my phone and scheduling software? +

We build on top of the phone line, scheduling, and CRM tools you already use — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or whatever runs your shop. The text-back logs into the same place your team already looks, so there's no second inbox to babysit.
