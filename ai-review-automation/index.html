Where we're honest with you

What AI review automation won't do.

We build this the honest way — and we'd rather you know the edges than be surprised by them. Here's where the line sits.

01 The honest limit It won't fake or write reviews.

Every review comes from a real customer about a real job

The automation handles the ask and the link — nothing else. It never writes, edits, or invents a review, and we won't build a system that does. Fake reviews break Google's rules and can get your listing penalized. Honest requests, sent consistently, are what actually lift a rating — and they hold up.

02 The honest limit It can't fix the underlying work.

It amplifies a good reputation — it doesn't manufacture one

If the work is genuinely good, automation makes that visible by getting more of your happy customers to say so. If there's a real service problem, more review requests will surface it, not hide it — which is honestly the point. This tool rewards a business that's doing right by its customers; it isn't a way to paper over one that isn't.

03 The honest limit Sometimes a simple habit is enough.

If a manual routine already works, we'll say so

A few shops already have a tech who reliably asks and a link in every invoice — and it's working. If that's you, you may not need this at all, and we'll tell you on the free call. We make our money on great fits, not on selling automation a business is already handling fine.

Common questions

What owners ask about AI review automation.

What is AI review automation? +

It's an automation that asks your customers for a Google review for you. After a job is marked complete, it sends the customer a short, friendly message with a one-tap link to leave a review — at the moment they're most likely to be happy. You stop relying on your team to remember, and your review count grows steadily on its own.

When does it ask the customer for a review? +

Right after the work is done and signed off — while the experience is fresh and the customer is happiest. We tune the timing to your jobs: sometimes that's an hour after completion, sometimes the next morning. The right moment is the whole reason automation beats a team member trying to remember three days later.

Will it post fake reviews or write them for customers? +

No, and we wouldn't build that. Every review is written by a real customer about a real job. The automation only handles the ask and the link — it never writes, edits, or fabricates a review. Fake reviews violate Google's policies and put your listing at risk; honest review requests are what actually move your rating.

What happens if a customer is unhappy? +

We can set it up so an unhappy customer is routed to you privately first — a quick "how did we do?" that catches a problem before it becomes a one-star review. That gives you the chance to make it right. Public review requests go to the customers who had a good experience, the way a thoughtful owner would ask in person.

Will it work with my CRM and scheduling software? +

We build on top of the CRM, scheduling, and dispatch tools you already run — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or whatever you use. It triggers off a completed job in the system your team already updates, so there's nothing new for anyone to remember to do.

How much does AI review automation cost? +
