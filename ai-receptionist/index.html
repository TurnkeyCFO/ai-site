Where we're honest with you

What an AI receptionist won't do.

We'd rather you know the edges up front. An AI receptionist is a sharp tool for a specific job — not a replacement for the people who run your business. Here's where the line sits.

01 The honest limit It doesn't replace your office manager.

It handles the front line, not the judgment calls

The receptionist takes the repetitive, high-volume calls — booking, basic questions, after-hours overflow — so your people are free for the work that actually needs a human. A tense customer, an unusual job, a pricing exception: those still belong to your team, and the AI hands them off cleanly instead of guessing.

02 The honest limit It works inside boundaries you set.

When it's unsure, it takes a message — it doesn't improvise

We define exactly what the receptionist is allowed to do: which jobs it can book, what it can quote, what it must escalate. Outside those lines it does the safe thing — takes a clean message, flags it, and routes it to a person. It is never set loose to make promises your business can't keep.

03 The honest limit Sometimes AI isn't the answer.

If a person on the phone is the better fix, we'll say so

Plenty of our first calls end with us saying the real problem is a missed-call text-back, a better voicemail routine, or one part-time hire — not an AI receptionist at all. We make our money on great fits. If this isn't one for you, you'll hear that on the free call.

Common questions

What owners ask about an AI receptionist.

Will an AI receptionist sound robotic to my customers? +

It answers in a natural voice and follows the way your team already talks to callers. We tune the greeting, the questions it asks, and the tone on your real calls before it ever takes a live one. Most callers simply get their question answered and their job booked — which is all they wanted.

Can it tell a real emergency from a routine call? +

Yes. We train it on your definition of an emergency — no heat in winter, a burst pipe, a gas smell, a lockout at midnight. True emergencies are routed to your on-call person right away; everything else is booked into the next open window.

Does it replace my office manager? +

No. It handles the repetitive front-line calls — booking, basic questions, after-hours overflow — so the people in your office spend their time on the work that needs a human. The judgment calls still belong to your team; the AI hands those off.

Will it work with my scheduling and CRM software? +

We build on top of the dispatch, scheduling, and CRM tools you already run — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or whatever you use. The receptionist books into the same calendar your team already lives in. We make your stack smarter, not replace it.

What happens when the AI receptionist isn't sure? +

It does the honest thing: it takes a clean message, flags it, and routes it to a human. We set the boundaries with you, so it only acts inside what it's good at and hands off the rest. You always have a fallback path.

How much does an AI receptionist cost? +

It's part of one flat monthly plan that covers every tool we build for you — unlimited agents, unlimited revisions, monitoring, and maintenance. There's no setup fee and no per-call charge. The free 15-minute call is where we figure out whether it's a fit for your business.
