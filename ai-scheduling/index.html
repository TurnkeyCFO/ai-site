Where we're honest with you

What AI scheduling won't do.

It's a strong tool for a specific job — not a replacement for the person who runs your board. Here's where the line sits.

01 The honest limit It doesn't replace your dispatcher.

It handles the routine bookings, not the judgment calls

The exceptions — squeezing in an emergency, re-routing a crew when a job runs long, handling the customer who's upset — still belong to a person who can read the situation. The assistant takes the predictable, high-volume scheduling so your dispatcher has room for the calls that actually need them.

02 The honest limit It's only as good as your rules.

It books inside the constraints you give it — nothing more

If your service areas, crew skills, and drive-time buffers aren't clear, the assistant can't guess them. Part of our setup is getting those rules written down properly — which is often worth doing on its own. Once they're set, it books reliably inside them and escalates anything outside the lines.

03 The honest limit Sometimes the tool you run isn't the bottleneck.

If a process tweak fixes it, we'll say so

Some shops don't have a scheduling problem — they have a "no one confirms the appointment" problem, or a calendar that isn't really kept up to date. On the free call we'll tell you if a simpler change gets you most of the way. We make our money on great fits, not on selling automation you don't need.

What owners ask about AI scheduling.

What is an AI scheduling assistant? +

It's an automation that handles booking for you — it takes the details of a job, checks your real availability, and puts the appointment on the right tech's calendar in the dispatch tool you already use. It also confirms the appointment, sends reminders, and can fill a cancellation from your waitlist, so the office isn't playing calendar Tetris all day.

Will it respect drive time and which tech can do the job? +

Yes. We set it up around your real rules — service area, crew skills, drive time between jobs, and how much buffer you like. It won't book a tune-up across town with ten minutes to get there, and it won't put a job on a tech who isn't certified for it. You define the guardrails; it books inside them.

Does it replace my dispatcher? +

No. It takes the routine, repetitive booking and confirming off your dispatcher's plate so they can focus on the judgment calls — squeezing in an emergency, re-routing a crew, handling the customer who needs a human. The AI handles the predictable scheduling; your dispatcher handles the exceptions.

Will it work with my scheduling and CRM software? +

We build on top of the dispatch, scheduling, and CRM tools you already run — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber, or whatever you use. The assistant books into the same calendar your team already lives in, so there's no second system and no double-entry.

How does it cut down on no-shows? +

It sends confirmations when the job is booked and reminders before the appointment, and it makes rescheduling a one-tap reply instead of a phone-tag chore. When a customer cancels, it can offer the open slot to the next person on your waitlist automatically — so a hole in the day gets refilled instead of sitting empty.
